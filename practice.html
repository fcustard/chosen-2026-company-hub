5 MINUTES AT HOME

Character Quest

Pick a scene. Try one small acting task. Earn a star for each task you try. You do not need a speaking line.

There are no scores for being “good.” Try, learn, and try again.

1. Choose your scene

Loading your scenes…

Read this scenePractice lines

2. Try a task

Do one task today. Try it again another day.

01 · STORY

Know what you want

Read your scene. Where are you? Who is with you? What does your person want right now?

Say one short answer out loud. If you have no lines, pick one person or task to focus on.

02 · LISTEN

See the change

Find a moment when something changes. What do you see or hear? Show one small, clear reaction.

Try it twice. Let the other person or the story change what you do. You can do this with no words.

03 · STAGE

Make it clear

Use your real scene words or action. Speak so the back row can hear. Move so the story is easy to see.

Do it once. Make one small change. Do it again. For a silent role, use your task, place, and reaction.

Stars mean you tried the tasks. They are not a grade. Saved on this device only.

3. Check in at rehearsal

Show this card to your coach. Pick one scene you worked on. Together, mark what the coach saw today.

I know what my person wants
I listen and react
My voice or action is clear on stage

My rehearsal checks

This is a simple check, not an acting grade. It stays on this device. Your coach cannot see it on another phone. A parent or coach may help a young actor.
