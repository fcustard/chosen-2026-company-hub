CURRENT REHEARSAL PLAN · NOVEMBER 1, 2026

Scene 11: “Silent Night” and the birth reveal

Mary exits with Anna while Joseph prepares the manger to instrumental “Silent Night.” A dedicated dresser removes Mary's prosthetic. Mary returns unseen and is seated with the swaddled stage doll before the baby cry and warm light reveal her.

Read Scene 11 · Scene 11 PDF · Read Scene 12 · Scene 10 change plan · All resources

The cry reveals Mary already seated.

The cry does not start her walk back onstage. Keep the instrumental, birth reveal, held silence and later vocal entrance as distinct parts of one musical sequence. Earlier illustrated overview PDFs contain superseded Scenes 9–11 instructions; use the current scripts and cue plans.

What happens onstage

  1. Keep the reassurance. Anna tells Mary, “You won't be alone.” Mary thanks her. Preserve Mary's “Joseph” and his “I'm here,” with their shared hand moment before the exit.
  2. Mary and Anna leave; the instrumental begins. On SC11-A, Anna helps Mary through the agreed masked same-side opening. Joseph remains in the shelter. Begin a sparse instrumental “Silent Night,” with no vocals.
  3. Joseph prepares a place. He carefully cleans the stationary manger and arranges the clean lining. His actions show care; he occasionally looks toward Mary's exit. Hold a quiet shelter image on LED 11B.
  4. Complete QC02 offstage. A dedicated dresser removes W01, adjusts the approved birth look and journey layers, and checks mic placement. Anna accompanies Mary in character. Store W01 in its labeled covered bag, away from the clean doll and cloth.
  5. Return unseen and settle. Wardrobe confirms costume readiness and Sound confirms mic readiness. On Stage Management's SC11-B, Mary and Anna return by the concealed route. Mary sits, then Anna passes her the one swaddled stage doll from the clean same-side preset and clears the family image.
  6. Reveal the family. The backstage lead confirms Mary is seated and holding the doll securely. On SC11-C, fade the instrumental to silence, then cue the baby cry and small warm stage reveal. Joseph turns toward Mary and the child. Keep the LED subdued.
  7. Protect the silence, then sing. Hold the family moment and Mary's written handoff to Joseph. Only then call SC11-D for the first vocal “Silent Night.” Continue into Scene 12 without restarting the pre-birth instrumental or repeating the cry.

Sound, screen and readiness

SC11-A — Instrumental / change and manger cover
As Mary and Anna exit after “I'm here.” Soft piano with restrained strings is the arrangement direction. No vocals or large climax. Build a gentle repeatable middle that can continue until the change and concealed return are ready.
SC11-B — Concealed return
SM GO after Wardrobe and Sound readiness. Keep focus on Joseph while Mary returns unseen and sits. The instrumental may continue until the seated-position and secure-doll checks are complete.
SC11-C — Instrumental fade / baby cry / family reveal
SM GO only after Wardrobe, Sound and the backstage lead confirm readiness. Fade to silence, then cue cry and a small warm stage light on Mary and the child, gently including Joseph. No full-stage blackout or LED flash.
SC11-D — Vocal “Silent Night”
After the reveal, held silence and Mary-to-Joseph handoff. One soft voice enters; others may join gently. Continue the post-reveal vocal sequence into Scene 12. Choose the first singer and final musical timing in rehearsal.

LED 11B/11C remain environment-only shelter stills. Use stage lighting for the family reveal. Do not project childbirth, Mary, a baby, or the costume change. Save the fuller musical build for later; let the family carry this moment.

Costume, masking and the single doll

Assign a dedicated dresser and backup, a backstage lead, a Props custodian and backup, and separate masking coverage if needed. Anna accompanies Mary; she cannot be the sole dresser, masking operator and doll attendant at the same time. Check the private bay, performer route light and masking from front and side seats. Keep the stationary manger and all exit paths clear.

The single-doll chain continues: Anna → seated Mary before the cry → Joseph after the reveal and silence → lined manger with both parents during the post-reveal vocal “Silent Night” → Mary after shepherd recognition → Joseph for the public name and final hold. Keep the swaddle on the doll; no second doll or extra manger placement.

What still needs rehearsal

  • Create or adapt the instrumental and vocal arrangement, including the repeat/hold, fade to silence, and later vocal entrance. Playback assets are not yet produced or released.
  • Confirm the birth look, private bay, seated position, masking method, crew names and first singer.
  • Time prosthetic removal, layer/mic adjustment, concealed return, seating and doll handoff with the performers. Rehearse readiness calls and an extended music hold.
  • Check front/side audience sightlines and the full Scene 11–12 sequence before locking cue lengths or running time.

This is the current cue plan. It does not certify that the costume, audio, masking, manger or doll trial is complete.
