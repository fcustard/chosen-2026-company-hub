New to CHOSEN?

Find your call, get ready for rehearsal, and know where to ask for help. You can start even if this is your first production.

Your first step: choose your name.

Open Company, select your name, then open My Calls. Check your own report time, scheduled-through time, location, and instructions before you travel. Another performer’s time may be different. Calendar subscriptions are optional and can lag behind changes.

If you’re acting

  1. Choose your name in Company to find your role, scenes, and My Calls.
  2. Open your full scene script. Read your lines, the lines just before yours, and the stage directions. Stage directions tell you what is happening or where to move.
  3. Practice spoken cues in Lines: read the cue, say your line aloud, then reveal the answer. Lyrics stay in the full scripts.
  4. Watch the actor rehearsal playlist when your rehearsal leader has assigned a video. Bring questions and write down blocking or director notes at rehearsal.

“Off book” means you know your lines without reading them. If your role or material looks wrong, use Production Help or ask your rehearsal leader.

If you’re singing

  1. Check your personal call and scene assignment first.
  2. Read the lyrics in the full scene script, including the words and action around the song.
  3. Practice with a CURRENT rehearsal track when one is published for your material. Some tracks are still in development; do not substitute an older recording as the official version.
  4. Mark any lyrics, entrances, or cues you need to ask about and bring them to music leadership.

The Lines tool practices spoken dialogue. For lyrics, use the full scripts. For a missing track or vocal question, ask for help.

If you’re dancing

  1. Choose your name to check your scenes and My Calls; being in the dance ensemble does not mean every dance call includes you.
  2. Check your dance assignment and read the scene so you know the story around your movement.
  3. Use the dancer rehearsal playlist for assigned choreography, counts, and spacing. Use a CURRENT track if your scene has one published.
  4. Bring appropriate rehearsal clothes, safe shoes, water, and any items requested in your call. Ask dance leadership about uncertain steps or formations.

If a dance video or track has not been released, ask your dance leader what to practice. Do not guess from an unapproved version.

Common questions

Am I called, and when should I arrive?

Choose your name in Company and open My Calls. Use your personal report time and scheduled-through time when published. Check again before leaving home; an all-company event window or calendar subscription may not be your individual call. If your call is pending or a personal time is missing, ask production before you travel rather than guessing.

What should I bring?

Bring your script or notes, a pencil, water, rehearsal clothes and safe shoes, plus anything your current call requests. Arrive early enough to check in and be ready at your report time.

What do “cue,” “blocking,” “off book,” and “places” mean?

A cue is the line or action that tells you when to begin. Blocking is your planned movement onstage. Off book means performing without reading the script. Places means go to your assigned starting position and be ready.

What if I am late or absent?

Use the Report Absence / Late form as soon as you know. Tell the team which call you will miss. Review anything assigned while you were away.

What if I am confused or my material is missing?

Check your role and calls, then the current scripts and Resources. Ask your rehearsal or department leader, or use Production Help. Questions are welcome.

How do children check in and out?

A parent or guardian signs each minor in and out; ID may be requested. Keep your phone reachable. Minors stay with designated leaders and follow the restroom procedure. Check My Calls for the individual pickup time. Read the youth procedures.

What if someone is hurt or I see a safety concern?

Tell the Stage Manager or a Director immediately. Then use the Incident Report form as directed. Do not wait for a form response to raise an urgent concern.

For complete company procedures, read the accessible Company Guide.
