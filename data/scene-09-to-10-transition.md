# Scene 9 → Scene 10: approved Joseph preparation bridge

Director decision: Keep the scene change continuous and visible while Mary changes into her journey costume with a fitted pregnancy prosthetic. No full blackout or scenic reset.

1. Scene 9 ends with Joseph agreeing to leave as soon as they can. Shira offers to help Mary prepare; Mary exits with her along a safe, lit side lane to a private costume-change station.
2. Hold the familiar Nazareth market image. The Roman motif folds into a steady preparation underscore. Village packing continues around Joseph, who remains in a warm pool of light.
3. Joseph opens his satchel and checks their essentials. He moves too fast, adds one item too many, then sets a nonessential item aside. He looks toward Mary's exit but does not hurry her. This is a wordless character beat, not a second census conversation.
4. The music and ensemble action may vamp in short phrases for the measured change. Mary returns with Shira in her journey costume and prosthetic. Joseph closes the satchel and offers his hand; she takes it.
5. Scene 10 continues with the scripted friend-supplied blanket, provisions, and staff. The LED road animation begins only when Mary and Joseph actually depart Nazareth.
