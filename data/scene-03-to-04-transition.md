# Scene 3 → Scene 4: approved near-black handoff

Director decision: Use a controlled near-black transition, not a full blackout or scenery reset.

1. After Mary's final "Joseph...," hold her narrow special for one breath.
2. Fade the surrounding stage and LED to deep blue near-black. Keep a safe side-light path for Mary's exit.
3. Mary exits on the marked side route. The unfinished table stays in place. The Scene 4 friends and their small work props are preset; no furniture or actor moves in unlit space.
4. Raise ordinary daylight on the friends' marketplace-adjacent work area to begin Scene 4.

The stage manager calls the lighting/media focus change and Mary's clear. Test the hold length, side-seat picture, route visibility, and cue handoff in a measured rehearsal. Do not assign a scenic mover for this transition unless that trial reveals a specific need.
