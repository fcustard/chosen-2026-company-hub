CHOSEN 2026 Company Guide

Welcome to CHOSEN: The Story Before the Manger. Whether you perform, work backstage, volunteer, or support a young performer, your part matters. We tell a story of faith, courage, obedience, hope, and promise that prepares the way for the birth of Jesus.

This web edition presents the guide in readable sections. New to the company? Start with the short FAQ and practice routes.

Current calls live in My Calls.

If a personal time is not published or a call is pending, ask production rather than guessing. Rehearsal dates, regular hours, and show-day details can change. Get set up

Save the Company Hub to your phone. Choose your name on Company to see your roles, scenes, and My Calls. Open your current scripts, published music, and rehearsal resources. Know where to report an absence or get help. Join the official company communication channel through the Hub.

The published Hub is the current plan. An old message, screenshot, printout, or subscribed calendar may be out of date. Check your personal call again before you leave home. Do not assume a regular rehearsal window means everyone is called.

How rehearsal works

Before you arrive

  • Check My Calls for your own time, location, status, and instructions.
  • Review the assigned scenes, songs, choreography, or notes.
  • Bring your script, pencil, water, safe rehearsal clothes and shoes, and anything requested in the call.
  • Arrive early enough to check in and be ready to work at your report time.

When you arrive

Check in at the designated area. Put away unnecessary belongings, silence your phone, and get your materials ready. Be in the assigned room when rehearsal begins. The work may start with prayer, announcements, a physical or vocal warm-up, or the material itself.

While you work

Listen when others receive direction. Take notes on blocking, music, choreography, and director notes. Try, adjust, and try again. Ask when you are confused; when possible, save individual questions for an appropriate break. Keep side conversations and unnecessary phone use out of the rehearsal room.

When you are waiting or leaving

Stay available. Review material, practice quietly, take notes, and watch and learn. A pause in your scene does not mean you are dismissed. Before leaving, listen for final notes, write down what to practice, clean your area, return borrowed items, and check your next call. If you are unsure whether you have been dismissed, ask.

Our rehearsal standard: Be early. Be kind. Be ready.

Attendance and communication

Your presence affects scene partners, music, choreography, blocking, technical planning, and other people’s time. Communicate early when something changes.

  • Absent: Use the official Report Absence / Late form as soon as you know. Identify the call and provide the requested information. Review what you missed afterward.
  • Late: Report it as soon as possible. When you arrive, check in quietly and get ready without interrupting rehearsal.
  • Planned conflict: Tell the team early so rehearsal can be planned.

Repeated absences, lateness, or missed rehearsals without communication may affect casting, featured moments, staging, or participation, based on what the production needs to be safe and prepared. Use the official channel for each question instead of sending the same message to several people.

The CHOSEN way

Excellence means preparing and giving your best. Honor means speaking respectfully and listening when someone else has the floor. Belonging means every role matters, including work behind the scenes. Care means noticing and helping people. Fun, passion, action, and service mean bringing joy and helping solve problems without distracting from the work.

When something needs attention, tell the right leader and, when appropriate, help find a solution. Keep communication short, clear, positive, and in the right channel.

Protect the room

Bullying, gossip, discrimination, profanity, unsafe behavior, and harassment are not tolerated. If you feel unsafe or see something wrong, tell the Stage Manager or a Director immediately. For an ordinary creative disagreement, speak kindly one to one, ask a team lead to help if needed, and let the Director make the final decision.

Room safety

  • Tell the Stage Manager immediately about an injury or illness.
  • Keep aisles and exits clear. Watch for cables, wear safe footwear, and stay in assigned areas.
  • Report spills, damage, hazards, or unsafe conditions.
  • For an incident, alert the Stage Manager or a Director immediately, then complete the Incident Report as directed.

An online report does not replace immediate notice to a leader.

Children, youth, and media

  1. Check in: A parent or guardian signs a minor in. ID may be requested.
  2. Stay with leaders: Minors remain with designated leaders.
  3. Restroom procedure: Follow the established two-adult visibility guidelines.
  4. Check out: A parent or guardian signs the minor out. ID may be requested.

A parent or guardian must remain reachable during rehearsal. Check the child’s personal call for the current report and scheduled-through times; do not infer pickup from a general event window.

Before sharing photos or video

Do not post photos or videos of minors without the required parental consent. Do not reveal scripts, choreography, costumes, sets, or production surprises without permission. Adults and parents or guardians can record a Yes or No preference through the Media Consent form. If consent cannot be granted, notify the Stage Manager. Follow the company’s media guidelines and ask before posting when unsure.

Costumes, props, and backstage

Costumes

Keep each piece together and return it to its assigned hanger, bag, rack, or place. Do not cut, pin, sew, tape, mark, or alter a costume without the costume team’s permission. Report missing, damaged, uncomfortable, or poorly fitting pieces early. Attend fittings when called and bring requested base layers, shoes, hair items, or personal pieces.

Props

Handle a prop only when it belongs to your scene, you have been assigned to move it, or a production leader asks you to. Return it to its assigned place. Tell the appropriate leader if something breaks; do not hide it or secretly repair it.

Backstage and equipment

Keep backstage quiet and pathways clear. Know your next entrance, transition, or change. Stay in your assigned area and follow Stage Management during runs and performances. If you wear a microphone, follow the sound team’s instructions, protect cables, report problems, and return it as directed. Do not use technical equipment without training.

Before walking away, reset used costumes and props, report problems, and leave the path clear for the next person.

Technical rehearsals and performance

Technical rehearsals may stop, repeat, or jump between moments while lighting, sound, costumes, and scene changes are worked out. Waiting is still working: stay available and know where your materials are. Your call may include check-in, costume, hair and makeup, microphone placement, warm-up, notes, and places. Be ready at your own report time. Always check My Calls for the current show-week schedule.

Follow the approved costume, hair, and makeup plan. If assigned a microphone, arrive for placement and testing, do not remove it without permission, and tell the sound team about any problem. Write down notes, ask when needed, and apply them on the next run.

During the show

Arrive prepared, be present, protect your energy, and be ready for places. Listen and respond to scene partners. Honor the agreed staging, choreography, music, and direction. Do not add jokes, reactions, or changed choreography without approval. If something goes wrong, stay calm and support the company.

After the bow

Return costumes, props, microphones, and materials as directed. Care for shared spaces and complete any Stage Management tasks before celebrating.

One company. One story.

Show up when called. Prepare between rehearsals. Honor people. Protect the room. Care for costumes, props, equipment, and shared spaces. Receive direction, communicate clearly, and remember that every contribution matters.
