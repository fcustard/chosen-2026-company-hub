Scene 10: departure and costume change

Mary and Joseph leave together after Mary's final “Thank you” to Nia and Simon. Music and the road image continue while Mary changes. They return together, with Mary in her journey costume and fitted pregnancy prosthetic.

Read Scene 9 · Read Scene 10 · Scene 10 PDF · All resources

This replaces the earlier change cue.

Both performers stay onstage through the Scene 9 ending and Scene 10 farewells. Earlier illustrated overview PDFs show the previous cue; their Scenes 9–10 change instructions are superseded by this plan and the current scripts.

What happens onstage

  1. Finish the farewell. Mary accepts Nia's gift and says her final “Thank you.”
  2. Begin the departure cover. Sound starts SC10-A. The village opens a path; Mary and Joseph look back, take hands, and exit together through the agreed lit side lane.
  3. Keep the journey visible. LED 10A lets Nazareth recede, then LED 10B supplies a quiet road loop or hold. A few travelers cross with bundles and clear the return lane.
  4. Complete the change. Wardrobe assists Mary at the private W04 station with W02 journey layers and W01 fitted pregnancy prosthetic. Joseph waits at the agreed ready position. The music and road image can repeat as needed.
  5. Return together. Wardrobe confirms costume readiness and Sound confirms mic readiness. Stage Management calls SC10-B. Mary and Joseph return together; Mary sets the walking pace and Joseph adjusts.
  6. Continue the scripted journey. Lower the music beneath dialogue. Keep the humor, rest, fatigue, “Just stay with me” / “I'm here,” and approach to Bethlehem.

Sound and screen cues

SC10-A — Departure / change cover
After Mary's final “Thank you” to Nia and Simon. Warm instrumental traveling pulse; a restrained echo of “Still Yours” may be tested. Build a smooth repeating middle. LED 10A transitions once into the LED 10B road loop; do not repeat the town-to-road reveal.
SC10-B — Road entrance
Stage Management GO after Wardrobe and Sound are ready. Advance out of the cover section as both return. Continue the road image, then progress the journey's light and landscape with the scene.
SC10-C — Bethlehem reveal
On Joseph's later “Bethlehem” line, reveal LED 10D and resolve the traveling phrase. Save the full city image for this moment.

Use environment-only imagery, a steady horizon, and gentle cloud or light movement. Leave the center clear for the live performers. Mary's pregnancy is already established in the story.

Props and backstage handoffs

Joseph retains Mary's handmade satchel and the traveling staff through the shared exit and return. The smooth workshop offcut stays in its marked tray. Track Mary's farewell gifts, blanket, provisions, and later water/rest handoffs. Shira is not required to escort the change.

Use the agreed private change bay, a clear lit route, a named dresser and backup, and a separate ready position for Joseph. Confirm prosthetic closures, Mary's movement and comfort, and mic-pack clearance.

What still needs to be completed

  • Name the dresser, backup, wing coverage, and cue operators.
  • Time the full costume, prosthetic, and mic check with the performers. Rehearse the repeat/hold and the shared return.
