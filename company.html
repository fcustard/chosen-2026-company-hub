COMPANY ESSENTIALS

Be early. Be kind. Be ready.

Before rehearsal

Check the Hub before you leave home. Bring your script, notes, water, and anything listed for rehearsal.

Attendance

Going to be late or absent? Tell us as soon as you know.

Report absence / late →

In the room

Be ready to work. Keep phones silent. Hold side conversations during notes. Follow the rehearsal leader and respect everyone's time.

Honor + safety

Treat everyone with respect. If something feels unsafe or someone is hurt, tell production leadership right away.

Costumes + props

Use only the props and costumes assigned to you. Return everything where it belongs.

Children + youth

Parents and guardians: follow the production's check-in, pickup, and communication instructions. Youth stay with their assigned production leader during rehearsal.
