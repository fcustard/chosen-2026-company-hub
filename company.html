COMPANY ESSENTIALS

Be early. Be kind. Be ready.

Before rehearsal

Check the Hub for your current call, time, location and assignment. Bring your script or rehearsal material, something to take notes with, water, and the shoes or attire requested for your work.

Attendance

If you will be absent or late, report it as soon as you know. Same-day emergencies should be communicated promptly through the official production process.

Report absence / late →

In the room

Arrive ready to work. Keep phones silent during rehearsal, avoid side conversations during notes, follow the rehearsal leader, and respect everyone's time.

Honor + safety

Treat company members with kindness and respect. Report injuries, unsafe conditions, harassment or serious concerns to production leadership immediately.

Costumes + props

Handle only assigned production materials. Treat costumes and props with care, return items to their proper place, and follow Wardrobe and Stage Management instructions.

Children + youth

Parents and guardians should follow the production's check-in, pickup and communication procedures. Youth remain under designated production supervision during their call.

One source of truth.The Company Hub is the company-facing source for current rehearsal information. WhatsApp is used to announce important updates; check the Hub for the current plan.
