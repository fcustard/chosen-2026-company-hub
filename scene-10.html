CHOSEN: THE STORY BEFORE THE MANGER

SCENE 10 — THE CENSUS AND THE JOURNEY

Approximate Running Time: 5 minutes Musical Treatment: Roman motif + 2–3-minute journey underscore No full song

Characters: Mary Joseph Roman Announcer Shira Talia Adina Dafna Levi Malachi Caleb Ezra Nia Simon Villagers / Travelers / Ensemble Innkeeper

CONTINUOUS FROM SCENE 9

No blackout.

The Roman Announcer has gone. The marketplace immediately breaks into urgent motion.

The people who were gossiping moments ago suddenly have their own problems: routes, family lines, supplies, money, children, travel.

The Roman motif continues briefly beneath the chaos.

Joseph is no longer listening to the complaints. He is calculating.

Mary sees his face.

MARY

Joseph?

JOSEPH

We have to go to Bethlehem.

MARY

Bethlehem?

JOSEPH

My family line. The House of David.

Beat.

JOSEPH

That’s where I have to register.

MARY

How far?

Joseph hesitates.

MARY

Joseph.

JOSEPH

Far.

MARY

How far is “far”?

JOSEPH

Far enough that I don’t want you making the journey.

MARY

No.

JOSEPH

Mary—

MARY

No.

JOSEPH

Listen. You can stay here. I’ll register and come back as quickly as I can.

MARY

We just decided we weren’t doing the difficult things apart.

JOSEPH

This is different.

MARY

Why?

Beat.

MARY

Because this difficulty has a road?

Joseph knows exactly what she means.

JOSEPH

I’m trying to protect you.

Mary steps closer.

MARY

Then protect me while we walk.

That stops him.

Mary offers her hand.

MARY

Together.

Joseph takes it.

JOSEPH

Together.

PREPARATION

MUSIC SHIFTS INTO PREPARATION / JOURNEY UNDERSCORE

The village moves quickly around them. No long farewell sequence. Every friend arrives with a purpose.

Shira gives Mary a blanket.

SHIRA

Take this.

MARY

You need it.

SHIRA

I have another.

Mary looks at her.

MARY

No, you don’t.

SHIRA

Then I’ll get another.

A small smile.

SHIRA

Come back.

MARY

I will.

They embrace briefly.

Adina arrives with practical supplies.

ADINA

Food. Water. Extra cloth.

Caleb appears carrying far too much.

CALEB

And I brought everything else.

Ezra inspects the pile and removes one item.

EZRA

No.

CALEB

What?

EZRA

They’re going to Bethlehem, not opening a restaurant.

CALEB

Joseph?

JOSEPH

Listen to Ezra.

CALEB

Marriage has changed you.

Joseph keeps packing.

Levi approaches with a staff.

LEVI

Take it. Levi gives Joseph a traveling staff

JOSEPH

Thank you.

Levi does not leave.

LEVI

I still don’t understand half of what you’re doing.

JOSEPH

I know.

LEVI

Doesn’t mean I’m sending you out unprepared.

Joseph looks at him.

JOSEPH

I know that too.

They clasp forearms. No larger reconciliation is needed.

Malachi approaches.

MALACHI

Take care of them.

Joseph looks toward Mary.

JOSEPH

I will.

Malachi reads the old fixer instinct in him.

MALACHI

And let her take care of you.

Joseph looks back at him.

JOSEPH

I’ll try.

MALACHI

Good.

Nia and Simon slip through the adults.

Nia holds a small handmade item.

NIA

For the baby.

MARY

Nia...

NIA

It’s small. He’ll be small.

SIMON

At first.

Everyone looks at Simon.

SIMON

Babies grow.

NIA

We know, Simon.

Mary laughs.

MARY

Thank you.

She accepts the gift.

The preparations are finished before anyone is ready for them to be.

DEPARTURE

The village opens a path.

Mary and Joseph look back at Nazareth.

Do not sentimentalize the farewell. This is still the town that has just judged them, and still the home that contains the people they love.

Shira stays close to the edge. Caleb lifts a hand. Levi stands farther back. Nia and Simon watch.

A faint “Walk With You” motif emerges.

Joseph reaches for Mary’s hand.

Mary takes it.

They begin.

Nazareth does not vanish at once. It recedes.

Then the road takes over.

THE JOURNEY

The ensemble creates distance rather than literal geography.

Bodies, fabric, travelers, light, wind, and changing horizons form the road.

The LED moves from the recognizable Nazareth horizon into wider, less protected country. Slow environmental motion only.

Light shifts through morning, afternoon, and evening.

Moderate haze may make distance and moving shafts of light visible, but it must read as atmosphere—not magic.

The movement vocabulary repeats: PACK → LIFT → WALK → STOP → BREATHE → WALK AGAIN.

EARLY JOURNEY

JOSEPH

Still all right?

MARY

Yes.

They walk.

JOSEPH

And now?

MARY

Still yes.

More road.

Joseph looks at her again.

MARY

Joseph.

JOSEPH

I didn’t say anything.

MARY

You were thinking it.

JOSEPH

I was.

MARY

Think quieter.

Joseph smiles.

They continue.

Joseph studies the road ahead.

JOSEPH

We should reach the next stopping place before dark.

MARY

You planned that?

JOSEPH

Of course.

MARY

I was afraid you’d stopped planning.

JOSEPH

I’ve grown spiritually.

Beat.

JOSEPH

I haven’t lost my mind.

Mary laughs.

The humor is affectionate. Planning itself is not Joseph’s flaw; believing a plan can guarantee the future is.

The music carries them forward.

TIME PASSES

No dialogue for a stretch.

Day becomes night.

Mary rests.

Joseph covers her.

He checks the road, then catches himself checking it again.

Instead of waking Mary or rearranging everything, he sits.

Dawn.

They walk again.

More travelers pass in both directions.

The terrain grows harder.

The movement phrase repeats with more ground time between actions.

Mary begins to slow.

FATIGUE

Joseph notices immediately.

JOSEPH

How are you?

MARY

I need a minute.

Joseph helps her to a safe place to rest without taking over her movement.

He gives her water.

Then he looks down the long road.

JOSEPH

We should’ve stopped sooner.

MARY

Joseph—

JOSEPH

I should’ve found another way. More help. Something—

MARY

Joseph.

He stops.

MARY

You don’t have to fix every hard thing.

Beat.

MARY

Just stay with me.

Joseph looks at her.

Then, instead of scanning the road for another solution, he lowers himself beside her.

JOSEPH

I’m here.

Mary takes his hand.

MARY

That’s enough.

Allow real stillness here. The road continues around them for a few seconds while they do not move.

Joseph cannot make the road shorter, Caesar disappear, Bethlehem closer, or Mary less tired.

For once, presence is the action.

ONE MORE STEP

Mary takes a breath and prepares to stand.

Joseph offers support without pulling her up.

MARY

One more step.

JOSEPH

One more step.

They rise together.

A tiny musical fragment may surface—not a new number, simply memory.

MARY & JOSEPH

Walk with me...

They continue.

The journey phrase resumes: WALK → STOP → BREATHE → WALK AGAIN.

THE WARNING

Time compresses.

Another stretch of road. Wind. Travelers. Changing light.

Then Mary stops.

Her breath catches.

Joseph turns instantly.

JOSEPH

Mary?

Mary raises one hand: wait.

Joseph waits.

He does not pepper her with questions.

The moment passes.

JOSEPH

Was that—

MARY

I’m all right.

Beat.

She is listening to her own body.

MARY

But we should keep moving.

Joseph understands.

The underscore gains urgency without becoming melodramatic.

They move.

BETHLEHEM

The horizon changes.

Joseph sees it first.

JOSEPH

Mary.

She looks.

JOSEPH

Bethlehem.

Relief.

MARY

Finally.

They share a tired smile.

Then the city reveals itself.

Crowds. Travelers. Bundles. Families. Animals. Noise. Arguments. Doors.

The visual arc completes: COMMUNITY → COUPLE → VASTNESS → CROWD.

Nazareth knew them too well. Bethlehem does not know them at all.

Mary takes in the scale of it.

MARY

Oh.

JOSEPH

It’s...

MARY

Crowded.

JOSEPH

Very.

Mary looks at him.

MARY

We only need one room.

Joseph takes that as a solvable problem.

JOSEPH

Exactly.

Beat.

JOSEPH

One room.

He spots a lodging place.

They cross.

KNOCK. KNOCK.

A door opens.

JOSEPH

Excuse me. My wife needs a place to rest.

INNKEEPER

No room.

DOOR SHUTS.

Silence.

Joseph looks at the closed door.

Then at Mary.

Mary looks at him.

For the briefest instant, the old Joseph wants to solve the whole city at once.

Instead, he takes a breath.

JOSEPH

All right.

He looks toward the next door.

JOSEPH

Next one.

He moves.

KNOCK. KNOCK.

No blackout.

The next knock belongs to Scene 11.

END SCENE 10
